ALICE IN WONDERLAND 

ABOUT THE STORY:
ALice in wonderland is a fantasy story written by lewis carroll in 1865. It is about a young girl named Alice whol falls down a rabbit hole and discovers a magical world called wonderland. 

MAIN CHARACTERS: 
Alice, 
White Rabbit,
Mad Hatter,
Cheshire Cat,
Queen of Hearts,  

SUMMARY OF THE STORY:
Alice follows a white rabbit into a rabbit hole. She discovers a magical world filled with unusual creatures and exciting adventures. Along her journey, she experiences many strange situantions and meets different characters. 

CONCLUSIONS:
Alice in wonderland is an entertaining story that teaches us the importance of imaginations, curiosity, and courage.
